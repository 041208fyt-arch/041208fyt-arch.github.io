1Q8 · Repricing Gap 重定价缺口

8. Consider the following balance sheet positions for a financial institution:
• Rate-sensitive assets = $250 million; Rate-sensitive liabilities = $120 million
• Rate-sensitive assets = $90 million; Rate-sensitive liabilities = $150 million
• Rate-sensitive assets = $160 million; Rate-sensitive liabilities = $145 million
a. Calculate the repricing gap and the impact on net interest income of a 1 percent increase in interest rates for each position.
b. Calculate the impact on net interest income of each of the above situations, assuming a 1 percent decrease in interest rates.
c. What conclusion can you draw about the repricing model from these results?

RSA＝利率敏感资产（rate-sensitive assets），RSL＝利率敏感负债，NII＝净利息收入（net interest income）。

2Q31 · Insurer Ratios 保险公司七个比率

31. A property-casualty insurer brings in $8 million in premiums on its homeowners' multiple peril line of insurance. The line's losses amount to $5,520,000, expenses are $2,080,000, and dividends are $240,000. The insurer earns $400,000 on the investment of its premiums. Calculate the line's loss ratio, expense ratio, dividend ratio, combined ratio, investment ratio, operating ratio, and overall profitability.

财产意外险（property-casualty insurer）；房主综合险（homeowners' multiple peril）；dividends＝返给投保人的红利。七个比率里六个的分母都是保费（premiums）。

3Q8 · Underwriting 股票承销：包销 vs 代销

8. An investment bank agrees to underwrite an issue of 20 million shares of stock for Looney Landscaping Corp.
a. If the investment bank underwrites the stock on a firm commitment basis, it agrees to pay $9.80 per share to Looney Landscaping Corp. for the 20 million shares of stock. It can then sell those shares to the public for $10.60 per share. How much money does Looney receive? What is the profit to the investment bank? If the investment bank can sell the shares for only $9.25, how much money does Looney receive? What is the profit to the investment bank?
b. Suppose, instead, that the investment bank agrees to underwrite the 20 million shares on a best-efforts basis. The investment bank is able to sell 17.5 million shares for $9.80 per share, and it charges Looney Landscaping Corp. $0.32 per share sold. How much money does Looney receive? What is the profit to the investment bank? If the investment bank can sell the shares for only $9.25, how much money does Looney receive? What is the profit to the investment bank?

包销（firm commitment）＝投行先买下全部再转卖；代销（best-efforts）＝卖多少算多少、按股收费。核心问题只有一个：价格风险在谁头上。

1Q8 · Repricing Gap

一句话套路：缺口 ＝ RSA − RSL；ΔNII ＝ 缺口 × 利率变化。符号决定赚亏，大小决定多少；利率反向，影响反号。

头寸缺口 Gap利率 +1%利率 −1%
① RSA 250 / RSL 120+$130m+$1.30m−$1.30m
② RSA 90 / RSL 150−$60m−$0.60m+$0.60m
③ RSA 160 / RSL 145+$15m+$0.15m−$0.15m

逐步看 ②：缺口 90 − 150 ＝ −60；利率涨 1% → 多付的比多收的多 60 × 1% ＝ 亏 $0.60m；利率跌则反过来 赚 $0.60m。③ 缺口绝对值最小，动静也最小。

c（结论，三句）: A positive gap means the bank gains when rates rise and loses when rates fall. A negative gap is the opposite. The bigger the gap, the bigger the effect.（正缺口：涨赚跌亏；负缺口正好反过来；缺口越大，影响越大。）

3Q8 · Underwriting

一句话套路：包销（firm commitment）看约定价——公司收入锁死，价格风险全在投行；代销（best-efforts）看实际卖出——佣金按股数固定，价格风险全在公司。

a. 包销：Looney 恒收 20m × $9.80 ＝ $196.0m（两种卖价都一样）。
投行卖 $10.60：(10.60 − 9.80) × 20m ＝ 赚 $16.0m；
只能卖 $9.25：(9.25 − 9.80) × 20m ＝ 亏 $11.0m（利润 −$11.0 million）。

b. 代销：佣金 17.5m 股 × $0.32 ＝ $5.60m，只按卖出股数收、与价格无关，两种情况投行都赚这 $5.60m。
卖价 $9.80：毛收入 17.5m × 9.80 ＝ $171.50m → Looney 收 171.50 − 5.60 ＝ $165.90m；
卖价 $9.25：毛收入 17.5m × 9.25 ＝ $161.875m → Looney 收 161.875 − 5.60 ＝ $156.275m。

守恒复核：公众付款 ＝ 公司收款 ＋ 投行利润，两种情形都对得上 ✓ 价格从 9.80 掉到 9.25 少的 $9.625m，代销下全部由 Looney 自己承担。

4Q26 · Megalopolis Ratios

先备料：权益 ＝ 10,000 ＋ 5,000 ＋ 10,000 ＝ 25,000；总收入 ＝ 利息收入 21,000 ＋ 其他收入 3,000 ＝ 24,000；净利润 6,000；总资产 185,000。五比率都是这四个数两两相除。

比率算法结果
a. ROE 净资产收益率6,000 ÷ 25,00024.00%
b. ROA 总资产收益率6,000 ÷ 185,0003.24%
c. Asset utilization 资产利用率24,000 ÷ 185,00012.97%
d. Equity multiplier 权益乘数185,000 ÷ 25,0007.40 倍
e. Profit margin 利润率6,000 ÷ 24,00025.00%

杜邦复核：利润率 × 资产利用率 × 权益乘数 ＝ 25.00% × 12.97% × 7.40 ≈ 24.00% ＝ ROE ✓ 链条闭合。ROA 只有 3.24%，靠 7.40 倍杠杆放大到 ROE 24.00%。

加餐 f–i（收入的四个漏点，同讲义版套路）: f. 利息费用率 ＝ 11,500 ÷ 24,000 ＝ 47.92%；g. 拨备率 ＝ 1,500 ÷ 24,000 ＝ 6.25%；h. 非利息费用率 ＝ 2,000 ÷ 24,000 ＝ 8.33%；i. 税率 ＝ 3,000 ÷ 24,000 ＝ 12.50%。四漏点合计 75.00%，正好 ＝ 1 − 利润率 25% ✓
